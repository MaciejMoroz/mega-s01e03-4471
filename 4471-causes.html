Why they were merged

Dedupe matches subject lines (decision 0001). "Wrong day", "invite is wrong", "dates mixed up" — both bugs produce those subjects. Fast during an incident, and blind to cause: reading the bodies is what separates them.

What the export adds up to

The export has 427 rows and 412 reports: the ticket system pages overlap and 15 ids come twice. Counted once, the 412 split exactly into the two causes above. Intake's guess (intakeTag) disagrees with the cause on 38 reports — the table's tagged as column shows both guesses; the cause comes from the tenant's locale and form, not the reporter's browser. Lessons

  • A big ticket is mostly one bug reported many times — but not always only one bug.
  • A count per ticket is honest only when every cause in the ticket has its own count.
